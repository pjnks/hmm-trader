Two distinct failure modes, two distinct fixes

BULL = Regime-Stationarity Failure (the "Bear Market Rally Trap"). HMM clusters short-squeeze upward volatility as BULL state. In a downtrending macro window, the model identifies mean-reversion peaks as bullish, then assets revert. Anti-predictive at top decile. A GMM-HMM swap will NOT fix this — the issue is regime identification, not emission calibration. Needs trend-conditional gating (e.g., 200-period MA filter on top of HMM).
BEAR = Gaussian Delusion (the "Fat Tail Confidence Hallucination"). Directional content exists (+6.9 pp above cohort base), but Gaussian emission slams confidence to 0.99+ on routine 3σ wicks because the Gaussian's tail probability evaluates to near-zero, forcing the regime posterior to overreact. Calibration failure, not signal failure. A GMM-HMM swap (Phase 3 of resurrection plan) fixes this directly via fat-tail absorption.

Implication for Phase 3 sequencing

The single-fix model from the resurrection plan needs amendment. Three viable paths:

  1. Pure GMM-HMM (cheap, ~5 LOC): fixes BEAR cleanly; partial BULL improvement via tighter confidence. Worth trying per cheap-fix-first principle.
  2. GMM-HMM + asymmetric position sizing: only act on BEAR signals (which carry signal); use BULL signals only for exit timing. Acknowledges asymmetric failure.
  3. GMM-HMM + trend-conditional gating: only generate BULL signals when a longer-window trend filter agrees. Filters out the bear-market-rally pathology.

The Meta-Pattern

The architecture worked precisely because it forced the team to stop asking "Is Sprint 18 better than Sprint 19?" and forced the question: "Is there enough gross alpha accessible from this asset class to pay for the physics of trading it?"

The journey arc visible in retrospect:

  • March: assumed HMM-on-OHLCV-of-major-assets was the strategy.
  • April 23: forensic discovery that the shadow ledger was frictionless. Type 1 vs Type 2 telemetry distinction enters the vocabulary.
  • April 28: Sprint 19 catastrophe-overshoot diagnosis. Polling-latency tax quantified at 2.07×.
  • May 4: drift-cohort pattern confirmed live (11 stops, 100% from conf ≥ 0.95).

  • May 13-15: strict cohort flipped net-negative at small N. Strategy Viability Frontier framework formalized.

This isn't a project failure. The strategies that were running on top of this architecture are mostly running on thin or negative net alpha. The architecture itself — the discipline, the harness pattern, the friction-honest accounting, the freeze-window protocol — is the real asset. The May 26 review evaluates not whether CITRINE survives, but where to deploy this architecture next.
